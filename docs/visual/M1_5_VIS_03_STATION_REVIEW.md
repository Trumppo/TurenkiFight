# M1.5 VIS-03 — Turenki station environment refinement

2026-10-31 graphics-session checkpoint.

## Scope

The retro-only 320 × 180 station SVG retains the recognisable ochre timber station, pitched slate roof, TURENKI sign, annex, canopy and warm platform edge. This pass adds a platform clock, gutters, service placard, restrained roof highlights and two short edge markings.

All new detail is above the active fighting plane or at its extreme sides. The middle of the platform stays a flat, low-contrast surface for fighter silhouette and hit readability. No simulation, input, camera, classic-mode or HUD code was changed.
